What this fragment is

A minimal complete experience taken from metic. – Take a closer look: a printed specimen sheet with a lens docked top-right. Press and hold on the sheet — or drag the dock — and the lens lifts: the sheet beneath it is scaled 1.6× about the pointer, and only inside the lens circle does the handwritten ink layer surface. Release and the lens parks back at its dock, the ink hides again. Notes read through the lens are ticked in the legend.
